REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL DEMO-NS-CLN-0426
Cleaning Services for Federal Offices in Halifax, Truro and Kentville, Nova Scotia
Issued by: Atlantic Federal Facilities Office (fictional)

SECTION 1 - PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 The Atlantic Federal Facilities Office requires routine commercial office cleaning at three office locations in Nova Scotia for an initial term of two years.
1.2 Services include nightly cleaning of workspaces, washrooms and kitchens, waste and recycling removal, floor care, and supply of cleaning products and equipment.

SECTION 2 - SERVICE LOCATIONS AND REQUIRED LABOUR
2.1 Halifax: The contractor must provide 60 labour hours weekly of commercial office cleaning at the Halifax office (fictional address: 1 Harbourfront Way, Halifax).
2.2 Truro: The contractor must provide 20 labour hours weekly of commercial office cleaning at the Truro office (fictional address: 5 Salmon River Road, Truro).
2.3 Kentville: The contractor must provide 15 labour hours weekly of commercial office cleaning at the Kentville office (fictional address: 12 Orchard Lane, Kentville).
2.4 Labour hours are location-specific. Hours delivered at one location do not reduce the hours required at any other location.

SECTION 3 - MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Each proposed delivery company, including any subcontractor, must provide evidence of a valid CleanSafe Level 2 certification with its proposal.
3.2 The bidder must hold commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 and provide a certificate of insurance before contract award. This requirement applies to the bidder as prime contractor.

SECTION 4 - SUBCONTRACTING
4.1 Subcontracting is permitted only where the bidder names each proposed subcontractor in its proposal and the Contracting Authority provides written approval of each named subcontractor before contract award.
4.2 The bidder remains solely responsible as prime contractor for all work performed by subcontractors.
4.3 This solicitation does not address joint ventures or consortium bids.

SECTION 5 - EVALUATION (POINT-RATED CRITERIA)
5.1 Use of certified green cleaning products: up to 20 points.
5.2 Relevant experience cleaning office buildings in the last five years: up to 30 points.
5.3 Price: 50 points.

SECTION 6 - KEY DATES
6.1 Questions must be submitted in writing no later than seven calendar days before the closing date.
6.2 The closing date and time are stated in the tender notice. Late proposals will not be accepted.
